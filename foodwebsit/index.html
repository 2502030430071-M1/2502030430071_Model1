Our Story

Food That Feels Like
Home, Right on Campus

Big Tummy has been the heartbeat of Silver Oak University since 2015. We believe every student, faculty member, and guest deserves a satisfying, wholesome meal that powers their day. Our kitchen blends classic Indian comfort food with global inspirations — all at campus-friendly prices.

100% Fresh Daily

Sourced every morning from local markets. No preservatives, ever.

Open All Day

Breakfast, lunch, snacks & dinner. We've got every meal covered.

Student-Friendly Prices

Great food doesn't have to break the budget. Fair prices, always.
